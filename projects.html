Women's Studies History Collective

This is a project I worked on for my Women's Studies course with Dr. Lisa Fine. We studied the history of the Women's Studies program at Michigan State University. WHSC was particularly fun and challenging because we got to use a web publishing platform created for institutions with collections. This meant that the artifacts we came across had to be entered into our system with as much correct and useful metadata as possible. Research involved many trips to the special collections and a lot of time scanning original documents or using talk to text for certain ones to make them easily searchable.
